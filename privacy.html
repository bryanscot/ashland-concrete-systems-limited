Privacy notice

This website does not have a database, user accounts or a server that receives your form entries.

The quotation form

When you complete the form, your browser builds a text message from what you typed (name, phone number, product, quantity, location and message) and opens WhatsApp with that message ready. The message is only sent if you press Send in WhatsApp. Once sent, it is handled by WhatsApp and by Ashland Concrete Systems Limited under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

Quote list

Products you add to your quote list are kept in your browser's session storage so the list survives a page reload. They are cleared when you close the tab and are never sent anywhere by this website.

Third-party resources

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Links to WhatsApp and Google Maps open those services only when you click them.
