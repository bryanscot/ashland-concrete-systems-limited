Careers at Ashland

Ashland Concrete Systems Limited seeks to recruit qualified, competent, result-oriented and self-driven individuals to fill the vacant posts below.

How to apply

1

Pre-register

Pick a vacancy and send your name, phone number and experience. It takes about a minute.

2

Prepare your documents

Gather the documents in the list below. Hands-on trade assessment and plant safety vetting happen at the factory.

3

Deliver and be vetted

Deliver hard copies to the office of the Corporate Head / Chief Executive Officer at Murang'a Industrial Park, Makenji, Thika, Kenya. Only shortlisted candidates are contacted and invited for interview.

Documents you may be asked to present
  • Factory employment form, cover letter and curriculum vitae
  • Copies of your academic, trade and professional certificates (testimonials)
  • National ID
  • KRA PIN certificate
  • Certificate of Good Conduct
  • Any licence or professional registration listed for the role (for example a driving licence with the right endorsements)

Online pre-registration helps us plan and does not replace the on-site process.

Vacancies

Machine OperatorRef REV/ACF/2/2026

The person is responsible for the direct execution, operation, and routine monitoring of hydraulic and vibration block making machinery. This role ensures the accurate pressing, forming, and initial demolding of high-quality paving blocks while keeping the production line running safely and efficiently.

Key responsibilities

  • Machine set up and operation.
  • Production and quality control to ensure uniform fill and density across all blocks.
  • Basic maintenance and troubleshooting through performing pre-shift visual and mechanical inspections.
  • Safety and yard coordination through operating within factory occupational safety and health guidelines.

Academic qualification

  • Minimum education: Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE) or equivalent secondary school certificate.
  • Government Trade Test, Craft Certificate, or Artisan Certificate in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Fitting, Plant Mechanics, or General Fitting from a TVET institution or polytechnic.

Experience

  • Minimum 1 to 3 years of hands on experience in a manufacturing, construction, or precast concrete factory.

Mechanical and Electrical OfficerRef REV/ACF/3/2026

The officer ensures zero unexpected downtime across the entire automated or semi-automated production line.

Key responsibilities

  • Preventive and corrective maintenance on vibration and mould assembly maintenance, batching and mixing plant upkeep and electrical and control systems.
  • Machinery operations and calibrations.
  • Equipment upgrades and fabrications.
  • Spares and safety management.

Academic qualification

  • Higher Diploma or Bachelor's Degree in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical/Electronic Engineering, Mechatronics Engineering, or Electromechanical Engineering.
  • Diploma (KNEC/City & Guilds or equivalent) in Mechanical Engineering (Plant/Production option) or Electrical & Power Engineering.

Professional certifications

  • Registration with a relevant professional body (e.g., Engineers Board of Kenya / EBK or EPRA Electrical License Class A or B is an added advantage).
  • Certification in Industrial Automation, Hydraulics, or Pneumatics.
  • Formal training in Occupational Safety and Health.

Experience

  • 3 to 5 years of hands on plant maintenance experience in a concrete manufacturing environment (Cabro/block making plant, precast concrete plant, or cement processing factory).

Equipment OperatorRef REV/ACF/4/2026

Equipment operators in a cabro plant drive the primary production cycle by moving raw materials into the batching plant, running the hydraulic block-forming machinery, and handling freshly molded and cured blocks across the yard.

Key responsibilities

  • Machine operation and monitoring.
  • Quality control and in line production.
  • Daily care maintenance and safety.

Academic qualification

  • Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE) or equivalent secondary school certificate.
  • Government Trade Test (Grade III, II, or I) in Plant Operation, Mechanical Fitting, or Motor Vehicle Mechanics is highly advantageous.
  • Occupational Health & Safety training: Basic certification in workplace safety, first aid, or safe material handling.
  • Licensing & driving credentials (for mobile plant operators): Valid Driving License with appropriate endorsements – Class I / Special Type Stamp (for Wheel Loaders, Excavators, or Tractors) and Class G / Specialized Vehicle Stamp (for Industrial Counterbalance Forklifts).

Experience

  • 2 to 4 years of proven hands on experience operating heavy equipment in concrete product manufacturing (Cabro, hollow blocks, precast elements) or heavy building construction sites.

Book Keeper / CashierRef REV/ACF/6/2026

To manage all daily sales counter transactions, maintain flawless petty cash and banking records, process factory operational invoices, and execute double-entry bookkeeping to produce accurate monthly accounts for management.

Key responsibilities

  • Daily cashiering and counter revenue collection.
  • General book keeping and accounting.
  • Petty cash and factory expense management.
  • Compliance and tax filing.

Academic qualification

  • Diploma or Bachelor’s Degree in Business Management (Accounting/Finance option), Financial Accounting, or Business Administration.
  • Secondary education: Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE) with a strong grade in Mathematics.

Professional certifications

  • CPA (Certified Public Accountant): CPA Foundation Level / CPA Part 1 (Sections 1 & 2) minimum. Full CPA-K qualification is a strong advantage. (Mandatory professional accounting qualification.)
  • Alternative professional certification: ACCA (Applied Knowledge / Applied Skills level) or ATD (Accounting Technicians Diploma).

Experience

  • 2 to 4 years of hands-on experience in a dual bookkeeping/cashiering role within a manufacturing plant, construction materials supply yard, hardware distributor, or busy commercial depot.
  • Demonstrated history of managing high volume cash and M-Pesa transactions while simultaneously keeping general ledgers updated.

Acts as the financial controller and business administrator.

Key responsibilities

  • Cashflow, invoicing, receivables/payables.

Site and Security ManagerRef REV/ACF/11/2026

The officer safeguards the physical factory perimeter, high-value raw material stockpiles (cement, sand, aggregates).

Key responsibilities

  • Site access control and perimeter security.
  • Loss prevention and asset protection.
  • Environment, health, safety and emergency management.
  • Security personnel supervision and stakeholder relations.

Academic qualification

  • Bachelor’s Degree or Higher Diploma in Security Management, Criminology & Security Studies, Loss Prevention, Business Administration, or Military/Police Science.

Experience

Experience

  • Minimum 1 to 2 years of hands-on experience in a precast concrete plant, cabro paving factory, or active construction site.
