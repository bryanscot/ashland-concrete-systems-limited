About Ashland

Ashland Concrete Systems Limited is a concrete products manufacturer and supplier based at Murang'a Industrial Park, Makenji, in Thika, Kenya.

Our range covers cabro and paving blocks, hollow blocks and kerb units. Whether you are a contractor, builder, hardware supplier, developer or homeowner, you can send us the product you have in mind and we will respond with a quotation.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a price?

Pricing is by quotation. Use any “Enquire on WhatsApp” button or the quote form and we will respond with current prices and availability.

Does the form send my details automatically?

No. The form prepares a message and opens WhatsApp. You must press Send in WhatsApp to complete your enquiry. Nothing is stored on this website.

Can I ask about several products at once?

Yes. Add products to your quote list, set quantities if you know them, and send the whole list in one WhatsApp message.

How do I know which product suits my project?

Tell us what you are building when you enquire and we will help you confirm the right product.

Do you deliver?

Please ask about delivery and availability when you enquire, and include your project location.
