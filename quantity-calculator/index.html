How this is calculated

Paving: area ÷ (block length × width). Hollow blocks: wall area ÷ (block length × height). Kerbs: run length ÷ kerb length. The allowance is added on top and the result is rounded up.

This is an estimate only. It uses the product's stated size and does not account for joint widths, laying pattern, mortar or site conditions. Please confirm your final quantity with us.
